# Deep Reef 1.0.34 — fish motion, eyes and recovery

All ten fish have stronger lateral S/inverse-S mesh poses, separate signed C-turn poses, and paired textured eyeball volumes. Body and eye geometry are rendered offline. Turning eases through neutral into a C bend while retaining at most four body submissions, one tail and one fin. Native sprite resolution and the 25 dense frontal swim samples are retained. Width targets remain unchanged. Collision depth reserves the larger bend envelope.

Projected pitch now varies continuously with heading rather than reversing its sign at front/rear views. The full-circle production-transform regression measures a maximum adjacent submitted angle change of 0.691406 degrees, with positive scales throughout. The 2,560 native draw captures include straight swimming and both partial/full C-turn directions, at HD/FHD and both graphics backends. All tail collars overlap: fractional root error <=0.002936 px, integer root error <=0.685543 px, zero draw-order or caudal-phase failures. [Attachment results](tail-attachment-results.json).

The reported mandarin/copperband/Banggai pileup is reproduced at safe near-contact spacing in front of the white coral. The previous release cannot find an escape. Numerical contact padding now allows separating or tangential motion only when the real body cylinders remain clear. Inward motion, actual overlap, moving-neighbor contact and tunneling remain guarded. Recovery includes pure vertical and small corner legs, with an arrival radius that lets a short leg execute. All three species move away in the 15-second fixture, with no swept or sampled display collision; displacement is 170–317 design pixels. [Before](coral-pileup-before.txt) · [After](coral-pileup-results.txt).

The final High-density regression runs 5,400 production ticks over three simulated minutes with all ten species. Maximum unintentional stationary time is 1.367 seconds and sampled collision violations are zero. Six isolated six-second windows show low net displacement while maneuvering; none repeats for a fish in consecutive windows, and every fish continues swimming. This does not establish that every possible crowd configuration is covered. [Full movement log](three-minute-after.txt).

The quick suite passes native compilation, all 56 Python checks, Roku single-cursor movement/activity tests, traffic/recovery/contact regressions, CPU reference trajectories, exact collision equivalence, clam contents and rare births, night/surface effects, appendage continuity, metadata release, settings and Back, upright transforms, turn interpolation and pan precision. The final short-leg recovery and three-minute tests were rerun after the last navigation change.

The tracked ZIP is under 4 MiB and source matched. Total decoded all-variant image dimensions are 143.11 MiB versus 142.54 MiB previously; these are dimension estimates, not measured GPU residency. Unique body regions rise only 123 → 125 per species. Retained pixels stay at native resolution. Palette compaction preserves alpha exactly, with mean absolute visible RGB changes below four 8-bit levels; the mandarin retains its original colors. [Motion/asset audit](fish-motion-audit.json).
