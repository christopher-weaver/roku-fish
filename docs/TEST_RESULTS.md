# Deep Reef 1.0.35 — continuous C turns and fuller bodies

The reported turn jump is reproduced with the v34 renderer: an infinitesimal heading change at an atlas boundary can move the body centroid almost nine design pixels and discard more than half its coverage. Smooth physics alone did not detect this visual defect; the former image test inspected the neutral swim bank rather than the actual C-turn path.

The C bank now keeps 25 refined frontal samples, actual signed silhouette edges determine its envelope, and textured outlines fade over an opaque interior material. The former unconditional head counter-sway is suppressed during deliberate steering. Projected pitch still varies continuously through front/rear views, without runtime reflections or negative scales. [Before measurements](turn-boundaries-before.json) · [Production-path regression](turn-boundaries-results.json).

All ten models have fuller cross-sections. The copperband neutral frontal width/height increases from 13.7% to 30.5%, guided by the supplied photograph. Source X/Y model coordinates are unchanged, and native sprite canvas resolution is retained. Width targets and limits are documented in [FISH_ANATOMY.md](FISH_ANATOMY.md); they are visual approximations rather than biological specimen measurements.

The final quick suite covers 59 Python checks, native compilation, every species at view boundaries in both C-turn directions, advancing swim phase during a steady turn, tail-root attachment at HD/FHD on both graphics backends, sprite/metadata loading and release, collision equivalence, traffic and recovery, floor/cave/surface clearance, clam contents and rare births, night lighting, appendages, settings and Back, pan precision and heading interpolation. The wider copperband's floor fixture moves its clear pose ten design pixels upward; its former buried pose must now be blocked. Ground safety was not relaxed.

A turn uses up to four textured surface submissions plus one small interior mask, then the existing tail and fin. Straight swimming retains its former draw budget. Quarter resolution applies only to the white material masks, not to photographed skins. Estimated all-variant decoded textures are 146.78 MiB versus 143.11 MiB previously; this is a dimension estimate, not measured GPU residency. Palette compaction preserves every alpha pixel, with per-pass RGB errors recorded in [fish-palette-results.json](fish-palette-results.json). Some appendages receive more than one palette pass; the reported errors describe the final pass, not cumulative error against the source photograph.

The rebuilt ZIP stays below 4 MiB and is checked against the source. The optional v34 three-minute log is historical; this release uses the targeted checks above. No Roku is connected, so native GPU appearance, FPS and memory behavior still require device validation.
